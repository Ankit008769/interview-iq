# Data processing agreement: template

> **DRAFT. NOT FOR USE WITHOUT LEGAL REVIEW.** This draft was written by the engineering team to describe
> how Interview IQ handles data for a college or placement office. It is not legal advice. A qualified
> lawyer must review it before it is offered or signed, including against India's Digital Personal Data
> Protection Act, 2023 and its rules, and any other law that applies to the institution. Square-bracketed
> items must be completed.

**Between** [Institution name], [address] ("the Institution")
**and** [Interview IQ legal entity], [address] ("the Provider").

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 The Provider operates Interview IQ, a practice platform for job interviews. The Institution uses its
campus features to:
- invite its students;
- set practice assignments;
- see each consenting student's progress, as described in clause 3.

1.2 Each student holds their own Interview IQ account. A student's practice data remains theirs; the
Institution sees only what the student agreed to share when joining (clause 3).

## 2. Roles

2.1 For the organisation's records (cohorts, assignments, invitations and the roster), the Institution
decides the purpose. The Provider processes them on the Institution's documented instructions.

2.2 For each student's own account and practice data, the Provider and the student are responsible as set
out in the Provider's privacy notice. [Counsel: confirm the role of each party under the applicable law,
for example data fiduciary or data processor.]

## 3. Data the Institution receives

3.1 Only for students who have joined the Institution's organisation and accepted the sharing terms
(version recorded with the date and time of acceptance):
- name, email address and roll number;
- which of the Institution's assignments they completed, when, and the score of each;
- the average of their recent AI-scored interviews;
- a placement-readiness index derived from their practice.

3.2 The Institution does not receive a student's answers, recordings, code, uploaded documents, projects or
reviews.

3.3 The readiness index is a practice signal for coaching. The Institution agrees not to use it as the sole
basis of any decision that has legal or similarly significant effects on a student, such as eligibility
for a placement drive.

## 4. Institution obligations

4.1 The Institution will:
- invite only its own students and staff;
- have a lawful basis for any roster it uploads;
- tell students that participation is voluntary.

4.2 The Institution will keep exported reports secure, use them only for placement preparation, and delete
them when no longer needed for that purpose.

4.3 The Institution will promptly remove staff accounts that should no longer have access.

## 5. Provider obligations

5.1 The Provider will process the organisation's records only to provide the campus features, and keep
each institution's records separate from every other institution's.

5.2 The Provider will apply security measures appropriate to the risk, including:
- encryption in transit;
- access control by role;
- logging of administrative operations;
- backups with tested restore.

The current measures are described in [Annex B].

5.3 The Provider will notify the Institution without undue delay, and within [72 hours], after becoming
aware of a personal data breach affecting the Institution's records.

5.4 The Provider will ensure that personnel with access to the records are bound by confidentiality.

5.5 The Provider will help the Institution respond to requests from students exercising their rights, to the
extent the Institution cannot do so through the product.

## 6. Student choices

6.1 A student may leave the organisation at any time; the Institution then stops seeing their data
immediately.

6.2 A student may delete their account. Their memberships are removed, and backups are reconciled on
restore so that deleted data does not return.

## 7. Sub-processors

7.1 The Provider uses the sub-processors listed in [Annex A], such as its cloud hosting provider. The
Provider will give [30 days'] notice of new sub-processors, and the Institution may object on reasonable
grounds.

7.2 When AI processing runs on models operated by the Provider on its own servers, interview content is
not sent to an outside AI company. [Counsel: align with the configuration in force at signature.]

## 8. Location and transfers

8.1 Records are stored in [Google Cloud, asia-south1 (Mumbai)]. Transfers outside [India] happen only as
permitted by applicable law. [Counsel: confirm.]

## 9. Retention and deletion

9.1 The Institution may delete its organisation at any time from the product, which removes:
- its cohorts;
- its assignments;
- its invitations;
- its memberships.

Students keep their own practice data.

9.2 Within [30 days] of termination, the Provider will delete the organisation's records, except where
retention is required by law. Backups expire on their normal schedule.

## 10. Audits

10.1 On reasonable notice, not more than once a year, the Provider will provide information reasonably
necessary to demonstrate compliance with this agreement. [Counsel: define scope and costs.]
